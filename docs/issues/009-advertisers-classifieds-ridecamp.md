# Migrate advertisers, classifieds, and Ridecamp archives

## Goal

Handle the community and commercial sections that do not fit simple news/event
models.

## Scope

- Migrate advertiser XML, sponsor pages, logos, and ad placements.
- Migrate classifieds categories, listing pages, and listing media.
- Migrate Ridecamp entry pages, archive indexes, and message pages.
- Decide which areas are read-only archives and which become editable NextGen
  features.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Advertiser pages and sponsor assets are available in NextGen.
- Classified listings preserve text, category, images, and status where
  discoverable.
- Ridecamp message pages remain addressable by legacy URL.
- Privacy and moderation concerns are documented for community archives.
